Attestation privacy policy

This service verifies the integrity of enrolled devices for remote verification and sends selected alerts. This policy covers its connection data, accounts, device security information and their retention. Reading this page does not create an account or enroll a device.

Purposes and legal bases

Connection data is processed to deliver responses and limit abuse. Account and device information is processed for the integrity verification you request, for account security and for the alerts you choose. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: the legitimate interest in reliable, secure verification, with data limited to these functions.

Remote verification is optional. The account and security information below is needed to use it. Verification results describe a device's integrity. The service makes no decisions about people with legal or similarly significant effects.

Information processed

  • Connections: your IP address, the request time, the path and protocol headers. Request access logs and request-related error logs are disabled. Connection and request limits keep their state in memory only.
  • Accounts: a username, a salted password hash, an enrollment credential, creation and login times, verification settings, optional alert email addresses and login sessions. Passwords are checked when you log in. The database stores only their hash.
  • Devices: a pairing-key fingerprint, attestation certificates, the verified boot identity, software versions and patch levels, verification times and security-state flags. The pairing fingerprint identifies a verification key. It is not a biometric fingerprint.
  • Security-state flags: screen lock, biometric enrollment, accessibility, device administration, debugging and other device protections. Biometric enrollment is a yes or no flag. No biometric samples or templates are sent.
  • Verification history: timestamps, verification strength, software and patch versions, verified boot information and the submitted security-state flags.

This information is not used for advertising or for profiling across services. The server has no endpoint for uploading samples. Local Auditor verification between two devices sends nothing here.

Cookies

The account interface uses one authentication cookie. It is limited to this host and set with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict. Sessions expire after 48 hours, or earlier when you log out. The cookie is strictly necessary for the account you sign in to, so no consent is asked (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). This page sets no cookie and runs no script.

Retention

A maintenance job runs five minutes after the service starts and then daily. It removes expired sessions and deletes records under these rules, on its next run after each threshold:

  • Devices you delete: after seven days, with their verification history.
  • Inactive devices: after 90 days without a successful verification, with their verification history.
  • Verification-history entries: after 180 days.
  • Accounts with no remaining devices: after 365 days without a login.

Deletion removes records from the database. To see or erase your account data, use the contact details above. There is no button to delete an account yourself.

Operational logs are kept in memory for at most one day and cleared on reboot. The service logs only critical failures.

Hosting and email

The service is hosted by OVHcloud in France. Account and device records are kept on encrypted server storage. Encryption does not stop the running service from processing them. Hosting and network providers can observe connections and keep their own security or business records.

Alerts are sent through mailbox.org in Germany to the addresses set for the account. They can contain account and device identifiers and verification warnings. The sending and receiving mail providers process the message and its delivery metadata. The provider of an alert address you choose can be in another country.

Mail to hello@diamaneos.de and contact form messages are received and stored by mailbox.org in Germany. Correspondence is kept while the enquiry and its follow-up are active. Resolved conversations are reviewed quarterly and deleted from active mailboxes within six months of resolution. A legal obligation or a legal claim can require longer retention. mailbox.org states that it keeps daily mail backups for up to seven days. Postal correspondence at the c/o address is handled by RA Matutis in Germany.

Your rights

You can ask for access to your data and for its correction, erasure or restriction. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. Identity checks are limited to what the request needs.

Right to object. Where processing relies on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to it at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). Send the objection to the contact details above.

Requests are answered within one month. Where complexity or the number of requests needs it, this can be extended by up to two months. You are told the reason within the first month.

You can complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, for example where you live or work. The website privacy policy covers the contact form and the other DiamaneOS services.
