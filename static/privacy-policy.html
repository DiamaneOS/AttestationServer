Attestation privacy policy

When you use remote verification, this service checks enrolled devices' integrity and sends selected alerts. This policy covers connection data, accounts, device security information and related retention. Reading this page does not register an account or enroll a device.

Purposes and legal bases

Connection data is processed to deliver responses and limit abuse. Account and device information supports requested integrity verification, account security and selected alerts. Article 6(1)(f) GDPR applies: legitimate interests in reliable, secure verification with data limited to these functions.

Remote verification is optional. The account and security information below is necessary to use it. Verification results describe device integrity. This service does not make decisions about people with legal or similarly significant effects.

Information processed

  • Connections expose an IP address, request time, path and protocol headers. Request access logs and request-associated error logs are disabled. Temporary connection and request limits use memory.
  • Accounts store a username, salted password hash, enrollment credential, creation and login times, verification settings, optional alert email addresses and authentication sessions. Passwords are verified transiently. The database stores their hash, not their plaintext.
  • Devices store a pairing-key fingerprint, attestation certificates, verified-boot identity, software versions and patch levels, verification times and supplementary security-state flags.
  • Security-state flags include lock security, biometric enrollment, accessibility, device administration, debugging and other device protections. Biometric enrollment is a status flag. Biometric samples and templates are not submitted.
  • Verification history records timestamps, verification strength, software and patch versions, verified-boot information and the submitted security-state flags. The pairing fingerprint identifies the verification key. It is not a biometric fingerprint.
  • Phones whose first pairing is not verified by hardware also store review flags from that pairing, such as an unusually fast response, and the reason for their latest failed check. These details stay with the device record. A phone retired after a reset keeps its record until 90 days after its last successful verification.

The service does not use this information for advertising or cross-service user profiling. There is no sample-upload endpoint in this server. Local Auditor verification between devices does not send its results here.

Cookies

The account interface uses a necessary authentication cookie, restricted to this host, with Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite=Strict protections. Server-side sessions expire after 48 hours and can be ended by logout. The cookie is strictly necessary for the account you sign in to, so no consent is asked (Section 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG). The privacy page sets no cookie and runs no script.

Retention

The database maintenance job removes expired sessions and applies the following rules. It runs after startup and then daily. Removal takes place on the next successful maintenance run after the threshold.

  • Devices marked for deletion: after seven days, including their associated verification history.
  • Inactive devices: after 90 days without a successful verification.
  • Verification-history entries: after 180 days.
  • Accounts with no remaining device records: after 365 days without a login.

Deletion removes records from the live database. Requests to access or erase account data use the contact details above. Account deletion is handled through that request process, rather than a self-service account-delete button.

The system journal is kept on disk with a 14-day retention limit, so the cause of a restart can be found. It holds system, kernel and login messages, not request data. The attestation service's own log stays in memory with a one-day retention limit, is cleared on reboot and is restricted to critical failures.

Encrypted backups

Encrypted copies of the database are kept in Backblaze B2 in the Netherlands (EU Central) and on an offline disk. They hold the account, device and verification records described above. A new copy is normally made every week and before changes to the service. Only a key kept offline can decrypt them. They are used to recover after data loss, not for ordinary account or device queries.

In Backblaze B2 the newest copy is kept. An older copy is deleted 60 days after a newer one replaces it, so each weekly copy is kept for about ten weeks. The offline disk keeps the newest copy and the newest copy of each month for one year.

Deleting a record from the live database does not remove it from existing backups. It normally leaves Backblaze B2 within about ten weeks and the offline disk within about a year. Provider processing can delay deletion by a few days. If a backup is ever restored, accounts deleted on request are deleted again. Devices you deleted yourself after that backup can reappear, and you can delete them again on the account page.

Hosting and email

The service is hosted by OVHcloud in France. Account and device records use encrypted server storage. Encryption does not prevent the running service from processing them. Hosting and network providers can observe connections and maintain their own security or business records.

Backblaze, Inc. is a US provider. Its EEA data-processing terms describe international-transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses. Backup content is encrypted, but the provider can process storage-account details, object sizes, timestamps and upload connections.

Contact us for information and copies of safeguards applying to transfers of your data.

Selected alerts are delivered through mailbox.org in Germany to the address configured for the account. They can contain account and device identifiers and verification warnings. Sending and receiving mail providers process the message and delivery metadata.

Contact messages, sent to hello@diamaneos.de or through the contact form, are received and stored by mailbox.org in Germany. An alert destination's own provider can involve other countries.

Contact correspondence is kept while the enquiry and necessary follow-up are active. Resolved conversations are reviewed quarterly and deleted from active mailboxes within six months of resolution. A specific legal obligation or legal claim can require longer retention.

mailbox.org states that its daily email backups are kept for up to seven days. Postal correspondence at the c/o address is handled through RA Matutis, Germany.

Your rights

You can request access, correction, erasure or restriction. Portability applies where its legal conditions are met. Necessary identity checks are proportionate to the data requested.

Right to object. Where processing relies on legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), you can object to it at any time for reasons arising from your particular situation (Article 21 GDPR). Send the objection to the contact details above.

Requests receive a response within one month. Where complexity or the number of requests requires an extension, up to two additional months are allowed, and you will be informed of the reason within the first month.

You can complain to a data-protection supervisory authority, including one where you live or work. Contact details above also apply to privacy enquiries. See the website privacy policy for the contact form and other DiamaneOS services.
